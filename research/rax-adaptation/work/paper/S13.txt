13 Summary Results of 1 The two simulation charts of 1 summarize RoboDojo, BEHAVIOR-1K, HumanoidArena,
DexJoCo, and LIBERO-Pro, and compare RAX with the strongest prior model, that is, with
learned policies such as VLAs and world action models rather than with other agents. For each
benchmark, the right chart pairs the average success rate of RAX, with each task at its largest
budget, with the average of the strongest prior model on the same tasks: VPP2 on RoboDojo, the
highest-ranked challenge entry on BEHAVIOR-1K, WB-WAM on HumanoidArena, ME-Dex on DexJoCo, and
VPP2 on LIBERO-Pro. On LIBERO-Pro, the chart therefore compares with VPP2 (42.3%), the strongest
model under position perturbation, and not with the coding agent ASPIRE (63.2%), which we compare with in Section 5.2.5 . On DexJoCo,
the chart includes only the five bimanual tasks, iPad, Hanoi, Assembly, Microwave, and Photo.
The six single-arm tasks are too easy to show the effect of scaling, since RAX solves each of
them within a few exploration episodes, and the bimanual tasks are the harder part of the
benchmark: with 32 exploration episodes, RAX averages 94.3% on them, against 48.8% for ME-Dex.
The average margin of 37.4 points stated in the abstract and the introduction is the mean of the
five margins of this chart, 53.4 on RoboDojo, 38.3 on BEHAVIOR-1K, 7.9 on HumanoidArena, 45.5 on
the bimanual tasks of DexJoCo, and 42.1 on LIBERO-Pro. The per-benchmark margins listed in the introduction instead compare with the strongest prior
result of any kind on all tasks of each benchmark. On DexJoCo, this is the average of all eleven
tasks at 32 exploration episodes, 96.7%, against ME-Dex at 65.3% (+31.4), and on LIBERO-Pro it
is ASPIRE (+21.2). Section 5.2.4 further reports the best session of each task across
budgets, which averages 99.5%. The left chart averages, at each budget, the success rates of the
tasks within each benchmark and then the five benchmarks with equal weight. A task without a
result at a budget shown counts with its result at the largest smaller budget. On LIBERO-Pro, 37
of the 40 tasks have a budget of 15 exploration episodes and use 11 to 15, Goal 1, 5, and 9 have
a budget of 20 and use 18, 20, and 19, and the 40 tasks use 14.2 on average (13.5 on Spatial,
14.0 on Object, 15.1 on Goal, and 14.0 on Long), so their results are included at 16. The dashed line, 41.2%,
is the mean of the strongest prior models of the right chart.
