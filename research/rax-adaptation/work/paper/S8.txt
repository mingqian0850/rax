8 Interface Contracts On the real robots the agent acts only through tools that wrap each vendor’s control SDK, and its
own programs call the same tools. Every command is checked before the robot moves; workspace, joint
limit, or IK violations are refused, and motion works only while the operator has armed the robot. Table 11 summarizes the tools; both robots also offer status and request_operator .
